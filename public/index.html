    How to Play
    • A Drag a letter Drag either center letter into a corner to add it to that corner’s word.
    • CAT Tap to score Tap a corner to submit its word. It must be a real word of 3 letters or more.
    • Longer words score more points
    • Earn a blank Every 25 points you score wins a blank. It waits in the center as a third letter until you want it — drag it to a corner and pick any letter you like.
    • Corners close A corner closes for good once no word can start with the letters in it. When all four have closed, the game is over.
